Tea is something to savor. It takes a little patience to brew (boiling the water, steeping your tea, letting it cool to a drinkable temperature) and, like any hot beverage, you can't just gulp it down. These snail tea bag holders are just the thing to remind you to take it slow and enjoy the moment.
