If you don't already know Telegram - it is an open, fast, free, and secure multi-platform messenger app comparable to WhatsApp (but without its dark sides, like gaining control over your content). Telegram has introduced the concept of bots. Bots are automated chatters that you can contact using a set of predefined commands.
