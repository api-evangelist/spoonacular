The Swirlio Frozen Fruit Dessert Maker is a pretty sweet device, pun fully and unapologetically intended. You can turn any fruit into a healthy frozen treat in seconds, and you can add any toppings you want after. Think about your favorite frozen yogurt place and go nuts (or go coconut, or go chocolate chip, or go Oreo pieces if you don't want to be too healthy...) Since the fruit dessert maker's parts are dishwasher safe, cleanup is minimal.
