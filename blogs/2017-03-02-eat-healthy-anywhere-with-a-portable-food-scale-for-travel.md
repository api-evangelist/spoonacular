Kitchen scales are great for portion control, but who has extra space in their kitchen? You can fold this portable food scale for travel or storage, so it fits any kitchen and you can bring your healthy eating habits with you wherever you go. To accommodate a variety of foods (and different measurement systems), this scale measures in grams, pounds, fluid ounces, and milliliters.
